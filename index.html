OUR STORY

SIMPLE INGREDIENTS. AUTHENTIC TACOS.
DANGEROUSLY GOOD.

Taco Heist started with a simple idea: make the kind of tacos we want to eat.

As a family-owned business, we set out to stay true to what makes Mexican street tacos so good in the first place—simple ingredients, authentic flavors, and food made with care.

For us, that means making as much as possible in-house. We marinate our meats, make our sauces and salsas from scratch, and prepare our accompaniments fresh. Every ingredient has a purpose, because we’d rather do a few things exceptionally well than overcomplicate something that’s already great.

No shortcuts. No unnecessary extras. Just warm tortillas, perfectly seasoned meats, fresh toppings, and house-made sauces you’ll still be thinking about long after the last bite.

So, why Taco Heist?

Because when tacos are this good, somebody’s bound to try to steal one.

Guard your tacos. Trust no one.
