Welcome to Westfield, Athletes & Fans! Refuel at Taco Heist This Weekend

Heading to Westfield this weekend for the Grand Park Men’s College Showcase (November 13–15)? Whether you’re competing on the pitch, cheering from the sidelines, or coaching between matches, Taco Heist is right down the road from Droplight Grand Park Sports Campus and ready to welcome you.

Located just minutes away inside the Westfield Collective (750 E 181st St), we’re serving up bold, authentic Mexico City street tacos and fresh, made-from-scratch flavors built to fuel a full weekend of soccer.

Why Teams & Families Love Stopping By

  • Fast, Crave-Worthy Fuel: Grab a Street Taco Trio on warm double corn tortillas (Carne Asada, Pollo Al Carbon, Carnitas, Campechano, or Pescado), build a loaded burrito bowl, or share Loaded Street Nachos and Chips with Queso Blanco & Guacamole with the whole crew.
  • 100% Gluten-Free & Nut-Free Menu: Every item on the Taco Heist menu is gluten-free and nut-free, making group meals easy for athletes and families with dietary needs.
  • Made From Scratch: We marinate our meats in-house and blend our salsas fresh daily—just remember to guard your tacos.
